About

Software Engineer with 3+ years of experience building distributed systems and production grade AI platforms.

At Technogenics (a subsidiary of StrikeReady), I design and develop event-driven microservices and large scale analysis pipelines for an AI-powered cybersecurity platform.

Alongside backend engineering, I work extensively on GenAI systems, including:
• RAG pipelines
• Multi-tenant AI agents (LangGraph)
• Self-hosted LLMs (vLLM, LLaMA)
• Guardrails, token-based quota systems, and LLM cost optimization

I enjoy solving problems at the intersection of distributed systems and AI, building scalable workflows and deploying high throughput services in production.

Tech: Python (FastAPI/Flask), Kafka, Redis, RabbitMq, ElasticSearch, Docker, Kubernetes, GCP, Angular, PostgreSql, Rasa, Quadrant, Chroma.

Always open to connecting with engineers, builders, and teams working on backend systems, distributed architectures, or applied AI.
